About

The Popcorn Bucket

The Popcorn Bucket started with a simple idea and a whole lot of hungry friends. Back in 2018, our founder, Chik Norris (yes, that’s really his name), combined his two greatest passions — crispy chicken and flavorful popcorn — to create a place where the ordinary becomes extraordinary.

What began as a small food truck parked outside local tennis courts quickly popped off (literally!). People couldn’t get enough of our one-of-a-kind popcorn creations, and soon, The Popcorn Bucket found a permanent home. Today, we’re proud to serve our community with bold flavors, cozy vibes, and a whole lot of character.

Our mission is simple: to bring people together over crazy-good popcorn and chicken, creating moments of joy, laughter, and a little bit of delicious chaos. Whether you’re here for a quick snack or a full meal, we believe food should be fun, memorable, and always worth sharing.

“Popcorn and chicken might sound like an odd pair. But one bite, and you’ll wonder why they weren’t together all along!”
